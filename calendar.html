Auction timing

Plan your next Treasury Bill bid

The Kenya Treasury Bill timetable is usually regular, but settlement still skips weekends and public holidays. Use this page as a quick planning view before you execute a bid.

Typical cycle

Auction day: Thursday

Value date: Monday or next business day

Maturity: 91, 182, or 364 days later

📅

Bid window

Most auctions occur on a Thursday, with bids settled in the usual CBK window. It is worth confirming the exact timetable when you are ready to place your submission.

💳

Settlement

Successful bids are typically paid by 2:00pm on the value date, not on the auction date itself. The calculator reflects this timing and the business-day adjustment rule.

🔁

Tenor planning

Longer tenors often offer a higher quoted yield, but shorter tenors improve liquidity. Use the main calculator to compare net return after fees and tax.

What to watch before bidding

  • Market direction: If you think rates are likely to fall, shorter tenors may protect flexibility. If you expect rates to rise, a longer tenor may lock in a higher yield.
  • Cash readiness: Make sure enough funds are available for settlement on the value date, including tax and any bank fees.
  • Tax treatment: Know whether your structure is exempt or taxable. The calculator can model both with a quick toggle.
  • Maturity date: Match the maturity to your expected cash need. A 91-day bill may be more useful for operational liquidity, while a 364-day bill is better for a longer cash lock.
Example

If the auction is on Thursday, the value date is often the following Monday. If that Monday is a holiday, the settlement moves to the next business day.
