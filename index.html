General Information

Minimum KES 50,000 for non-competitive bids, in multiples of KES 50,000. Competitive bids start at KES 2,000,000.

Include tax?
Tax rate: 15.00%

CBK withholds tax on the discount at settlement. Resident individuals usually pay 15% qualifying-interest WHT, which is generally final tax. Enter 0% if you have a KRA exemption certificate, or another rate if your status differs.

Use the CBK published weighted average rate, or your competitive yield, with up to four decimal places. Changing tenor fills the matching published rate unless you typed a custom yield.

Other Expenses

Optional cash costs that are not part of the CBK settlement amount: bank KEPSS/RTGS fees, broker charges, or VAT on those fees. Kenya VAT is 16%.

How the numbers are calculated

Treasury bills are sold at a discount. You choose the face value you will receive at maturity, then pay a lower price today. CBK publishes price per KES 100 from the quoted annual yield:

P = 100 ÷ (1 + r × d ÷ 365)

  • r is the annual yield as a decimal (8.7837% is 0.087837).
  • d is 91, 182, or 364 days. The year basis is 365, matching CBK.
  • Bill price = P × (face value ÷ 100). P is rounded to four decimal places, matching CBK result sheets (for example 8.7674% for 91 days on 14 September 2026 priced at 97.8609).
  • Gross interest = face value − bill price.
  • Withholding tax, when included, is 15% of that discount and is added to the amount you pay CBK at settlement. You still receive full face value at maturity.
  • Period return uses cash actually invested (CBK payment plus extra expenses), not face value. Simple annualized return scales that by 365 ÷ days. Compounded annual return is (1 + period return)^(365 ÷ days) − 1.
  • The quoted yield is higher than your after-tax return because tax is paid up front on the discount.

Investor facts

When do I bid and pay?
Bids are due by 2:00pm on auction day, usually Thursday, through DhowCSD or Treasury Mobile Direct. If you are successful, pay by 2:00pm on the value date — typically the following Monday, or the next business day after a holiday.
Non-competitive or competitive?
From KES 50,000 up to just under KES 2,000,000 you bid non-competitively and take the weighted average accepted rate. From KES 2,000,000 you may also bid a yield competitively.
What number should match DhowCSD?
Match Pay CBK today (bill price plus WHT). Bank KEPSS/RTGS charges sit on top of that and are your bank’s fees, not CBK’s settlement amount.
Why is tax added to what I pay?
The bill is discounted. CBK withholds tax on that discount at settlement, so you fund price + tax now and still receive full face value at maturity.
